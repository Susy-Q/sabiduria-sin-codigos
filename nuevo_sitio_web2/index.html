COURSE CONTENT

Five steps toward digital confidence.

A brief preview of what students will learn after registering.

01

My first encounter with AI

  • Taking the first steps with confidenceLearn what AI can do while keeping personal judgment and control.
  • The evolution of artificial intelligenceDiscover how computers, the internet and conversational tools led to today's AI.
  • The Robot Chef analogyUnderstand how AI learns from examples, recognizes patterns and can also make mistakes.
  • Basic concepts without technical languageLearn simply what data, models, training, responses and verification mean.
  • AI in everyday lifeIdentify AI in phones, maps, email, banks, photos and entertainment services.
02

AI for everyday life

  • AI virtual assistantsUnderstand how an assistant receives instructions, processes information and proposes useful answers.
  • ChatGPT for conversationUse conversations to ask questions, receive explanations, summarize and improve written ideas.
  • Google Gemini for informationExplore how Gemini supports searches, organization and work with different types of content.
  • Microsoft Copilot for daily workLearn how AI can assist with documents, email, tables and familiar Microsoft tools.
  • Choosing the right toolCompare assistants according to the task, ease of use, sources, privacy and desired result.
03

The art of conversation

  • What an AI instruction (prompt) isA prompt is simply the instruction or question you write to tell the AI what help you need.
  • Four parts of a clear instructionCombine the goal, context, desired format and tone to obtain more useful answers.
  • Improving instructions step by stepReview an initial answer and ask for changes in length, clarity, examples or organization.
  • Instructions for practical tasksPractice creating messages, summaries, tables, images, comparisons and personal plans.
  • Research and multimodal AIWork carefully with sources, images and documents while checking important information.
04

Safety and good judgment

  • AI hallucinations and errorsRecognize that a convincing answer may contain invented, incomplete or outdated information.
  • Verifying important informationConfirm dates, prices, health guidance and official procedures using reliable sources.
  • Privacy and personal dataLearn what information should never be shared with an AI assistant or unknown service.
  • Suspicious messages and scamsIdentify urgency, emotional pressure, strange links and requests for money or codes.
  • Manipulated images, videos and voicesUse observation and verification to recognize misleading or AI-generated multimedia content.
05

Creative practical workshop

  • Preparing a personal projectChoose a real need and follow the complete cycle from idea and prompt to review and final use.
  • Planning a safe tripCreate an itinerary with rest, transportation, budget and details that must be confirmed.
  • Writing important communicationsPrepare clear letters and messages, then review tone, facts and recipient information.
  • Organizing health information safelyCreate questions or summaries without sharing diagnoses, identification numbers or private records.
  • Scam detection and final projectApply verification and security habits in a practical result that can be saved and used.
